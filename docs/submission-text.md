## Project name
My Story: Abandoned but not Forgotten

## Summary (short)
An interactive storybook that tells the stories of five machines NASA left behind on the Moon, Mars and beyond. Open the book, answer one question, then flip to each machine. The page zooms into a NASA image and plays a silent story with typewriter text, timelines and scanner panels. Finish a story to earn its postage stamp.

## Detailed description
Spacecraft are usually remembered for launch and landing. This project asks what happens after: the quiet years, the last signal, and what the machine left us.

"My Story" is a web storybook for school-age and casual space fans. It opens as a book. On the first page the reader answers one question, and that answer comes back at the end of each story. Five pages follow, one for each machine: the Apollo 17 rover and science station on the Moon, NEAR Shoemaker at asteroid Eros, Opportunity on Mars, Dawn at Ceres, and Ingenuity on Mars.

Each page zooms into a NASA image and then plays a silent, caption-driven story, like a short video you can pause or skip. Scenes include timelines, scanner-style panels with labeled parts, number comparisons, and photo scenes. Each story covers what the machine did, how it ended, and what it changed for us. Reflective lines are labeled "STORY VOICE" and are never presented as NASA's or a machine's words.

Every fact comes from NASA or NSSDCA pages, and each scene carries a source tag. Where NASA pages disagree, the project avoids the disputed number or notes the conflict. Finishing a story earns a postage stamp that is stamped onto the page, and all stamps are shown together on the last page.

It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no backend. It works with a keyboard, with swipe on touch screens, and with a reduced-motion setting, and screen readers get the full text. The reader's answer stays in the browser and is not saved or sent anywhere.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.
